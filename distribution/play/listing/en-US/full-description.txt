One reader. Every device.

Keryx is a local-first, cross-platform RSS reader. By default, all of your data
lives on your device; turn on cloud sync only if you want the same subscriptions,
read/starred status, and articles to follow you between your phone and your desktop.

FEATURES

• Multi-device sync via your own cloud storage (Dropbox, Google Drive, or
  OneDrive) — no account with Keryx itself, ever. On Android, Google Drive sync
  requires Google Play services; devices without it can still sync via Dropbox
  or OneDrive.
• Local-first: works fully offline, with no sync required
• Fast local full-text search across article titles and content
• Organize feeds with tags (cross-cutting labels) and folders (hierarchical
  grouping)
• RSS 2.0 and Atom 1.0 support (RSS 1.0/RDF is also loosely parsed)
• OPML import and export, for moving to or from other RSS readers and for
  backing up your subscriptions
• Completely free — no freemium tiers, no trial period, no ads
• Open source — the full source code is public and can be inspected or modified
  by anyone

PRIVACY

Keryx has no user accounts and no server operated by its developer. There is
nothing to sign up for, and nothing that its developer's own infrastructure ever
receives. The app includes no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising,
or tracking of any kind.

Keryx talks only to servers directly relevant to what you're doing: the feeds you
subscribe to (and their favicons), GitHub for update checks, and — only if you
connect one — Dropbox, Google Drive, or OneDrive for sync. Nothing else.

Learn more, including the full privacy policy, at https://keryx.merc.works
